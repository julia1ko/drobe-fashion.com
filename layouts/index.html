The concept

Your body. Your closet. Your rules.

Most people own more clothes than they will ever wear, and every morning the same question returns: what should I wear? The problem is not the clothes. It is that we cannot see them, remember them, or imagine them on our bodies.

DROBE solves this with one core innovation: a 4D digital avatar that is an exact replica of your body. Every item you own lives in a virtual closet: digitised, organised, and always accessible. See your entire wardrobe on your avatar at once. Build outfits from what you actually own. When you need help, an AI stylist knows your body, your closet, and your taste.

When something no longer serves you, sell, trade, and discover independent makers, giving every garment a longer life.

Not about buying more. About finally knowing what you have, and always knowing what to wear.
